The CAGE questionnaire is, along with TWEAK, AUDIT (Alcohol Use Disorders Identification Test) and FAST (Fast Alcohol Screening Test), one of the most popular alcohol screening questionnaires. It was developed by Dr John Ewing in the USA and published in 1984 [1] as an easy-to-use tool to identify severe alcohol dependence. It is important that it is stressed that the questions relate to the whole of the patient's life, not just the current circumstances. Each question is scored 1 for 'yes' and 0 for 'no'.
