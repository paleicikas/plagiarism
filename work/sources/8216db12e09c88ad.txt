Chapter Three:Research design and methodology
In this chapter, the research methodology and research design are systematically outlined in accordance with University guidelines. The chapter focuses on explaining why the study was conducted, how the research problem was defined, the manner in which the hypothesis was formulated, the type of data that was collected, and the specific methods that were applied. Clarification of the rationale for the chosen techniques of data analysis is provided, and a range of related ethical considerations that typically arise when examining research methodology in relation to a defined problem or study is addressed (Goundar, 2012; Creswell & Creswell, 2018).
