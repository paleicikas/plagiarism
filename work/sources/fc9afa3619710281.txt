Written by Susan Dyer-Layer (Passages of Hope Director)
In our rapidly changing culture, confusion and fear abounds. Increasingly, God's truth is being silenced. Our teens and young adults are making the biggest decisions of their lives regarding life and relationships. Passages of Hope wants to equip you to reach our younger generation with the new Whole Truth About Choice message.
Why attend Equip to Heal?
1) Teens and twenties are the most vulnerable age-group for abortion.
Over 70% of the abortions in the United States occur in the teens and twenties age group. (Guttmacher, 2014). The culture's message "My body, my right" leaves our youth in the dark from the emotional consequences from an abortion choice.
2) Insights to reach our young.
We need to understand the culture to reach younger generations with Truth. What do they care about? What are they being told?
3) We are called to speak out.
Many Christian sisters and brothers walk through an abortion loss alone because of fear. Our culture is silent about the negative consequences of abortion, pornography, or homosexuality. If we are silent, where will our young generation go to find answers?
4) It's virtual!
You can sit in your pjs while taking in the invaluable insights of our speakers. If you are unable to join us, still register to receive a recording of the conference!
We are called to speak boldly for the glory of God. Be encouraged and equipped to speak out by attending to our Equip to Heal virtual conference on Saturday, January 23rd at 10 a.m. to 11:45 a.m. You can register at passagesofhope.org/events/equip-to-heal.
