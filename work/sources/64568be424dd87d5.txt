Toolkit: The Impact of the "Serial 
Shutdown" of EPA 
 
Introduction 
With another government shutdown underway, the chaos and disruption of the last 
nine months to EPA employees and the communities they serve has come into 
stark focus. As disruptive as a shutdown can be, it also may be a sneak preview of 
what will happen to EPA safeguards and programs if the Trump administration 
continues to have carte blanche to systematically dismantle and weaken the 
agency. Because Congress has not stepped up in a bipartisan way to provide the 
staffing, resources, and guardrails against executive overreach needed for EPA to 
fulfill its mission, the protections that safeguard our families' health, air, water, and 
communities continue to diminish. 
 
We've prepared this toolkit to help you take action. Inside, you'll find clear language 
you can use to explain the "serial shutdown" and why it matters, as well as social 
media language and posts you can customize and post to your networks. 
 
EPN's Role So Far 
Over the past week, EPN has been driving the public narrative on what we're calling 
the "serial shutdown" of EPA. This isn't just about one budget fight-it's the 
cumulative effect of undercutting the agency piece by piece: starving it of staff, 
resources. 
● Media Impact: EPN volunteers and experts have been quoted and featured 
widely in coverage of the shutdown's impact. Reporters are relying on your 

expertise to explain what this means for people in real terms-more polluted 
water, dirtier air, stalled cleanups, and higher costs for families. 
● Press Briefing: Our press briefing earlier this week drew strong media 
attendance, giving national, local and trade outlets direct access to EPN 
expertise. 
● Public Statement: We also issued a statement against the administration's 
threatened mass firings, which has already been picked up and amplified 
across social media.

Talking Points 
 
Topline Messaging 
● While we don't yet know how this shutdown will impact the day-to-day 
functioning of EPA, what we do know is that the administration has been 
dismantling protections for Americans with an ongoing "serial shutdown." 

Piece by piece, protections are disappearing, leaving families exposed to 
more dirty air, unsafe water, and unchecked toxic pollution. 
● When EPA is weakened or shut down, our children and families are less safe. 
Until this administration is checked by Congress, Americans can count on 
more kids with asthma, more families exposed to toxic chemicals, and more 
neighborhoods living near sites that go uncleaned. 
● Americans want an effective EPA. Nationwide polling shows voters 
overwhelmingly back a strong EPA-88% of all voters, including 81% of 
Trump voters, want Congress to increase or at least maintain EPA funding. 
 
When EPA shuts down, our protections do too-and families pay the price. 
● Without a strong, functioning EPA, families lose the protections that keep 
kids out of emergency rooms and cancer wards. Protections like drinking 
water oversight, toxic site cleanups, and air monitoring are delayed, reduced, 
or put on hold. That means Americans face greater risks to their health, and 
higher medical bills and utility costs down the road. 
● Pollution doesn't pause because of political fights-it keeps spreading while 
fewer scientists, inspectors, and public servants are on the job. EPA has been 
putting polluters ahead of people for months now by weakening oversight, 
and a shutdown opens the door to even more. 
● We've seen what happens when EPA shuts down. During the 2018-19 
furlough, coal plants actually increased particulate pollution by dialing back 
their pollution controls. Without EPA staff on the job, oversight disappeared, 
and polluters took advantage. 
 
The dangers of a diminished or shut-down EPA reach into communities across 
the country. 
● When EPA is gutted, protections everywhere are gutted. EPA's partnership 
with states and tribes is the backbone of America's clean air and clean water. 
EPA sets the national standards, provides the science and funding, and acts 
as the backstop when local agencies can't. States and tribes simply can't fill 
the gap when EPA's budget is shut down. 

● Tribal programs are especially vulnerable. Many tribal governments rely 
almost entirely on EPA support for clean water and air protection. Without 
EPA staff and funding, those programs can grind to a halt. 
 
We all lose when public servants at EPA are used as bargaining chips. 
● Behind every EPA program is a scientist, engineer, or career expert who 
serves the public by protecting the air we breathe, the water we drink, and 
the food we eat. A shutdown threatens these civil servants-putting their 
livelihoods, their research, and our health protections at risk. 
● With nearly 90% of EPA staff planned for furlough, this shutdown is yet 
another step by the Trump administration to remove career expertise and 
force through rollbacks that will harm millions of Americans while benefiting 
polluters. 
● This is not how civil servants should be treated. EPN stands with EPA staff 
who deserve respect, not political games.
