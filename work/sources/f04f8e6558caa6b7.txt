The CAGE questionnaire is used to screen for alcohol abuse. CAGE is an acronym and stands for Cut down drinking, Annoyed by criticism, Guilty feelings and Eye-opener.
 
 
 
 
 
 Interpretation
 
 
 
 
 | Points | | 
 | 0-1 | Alcohol abuse unlikely | 
 | 2-4 | Alcohol abuse or dependence likely | 
 
 
 
 
 
 References
 
 - Ewing JA. Detecting alcoholism. The CAGE questionnaire. JAMA 1984; 252: 1905-1907
