The Journal of the Association for Consumer Research (JACR) publishes quarterly thematic issues exploring unique topics in consumer behavior. The mission of JACR is to broaden the intellectual scope and interdisciplinary influence of the Association for Consumer Research.
The Association for Consumer Research (ACR) awards individuals for their outstanding contributions to the field of consumer research to acknowledge and celebrate excellence. These awards recognize achievements such as impactful research, lifetime dedication, and significant contributions to advancing knowledge in consumer behavior. By honoring exceptional scholars, ACR aims to encourage high-quality research, inspire future contributions, and foster a vibrant community dedicated to understanding and shaping consumer behavior.
ACR disseminates the latest consumer research insights in the Journal of the Association for Consumer Research, conference proceedings, its dedicated podcast "Consumers Insights 101," webinars, educational videos, and more! To support this ongoing research, ACR also provides grants and scholarships for research proposals. Members can also join the ACR listserv and social media sites (LinkedIn, Facebook, X) to stay connected.
Transformative Consumer Research is a movement within the Association for Consumer Research community that seeks to encourage, support, and publicize research that benefits consumer welfare and quality of life for all beings affected by consumption across the world.
